# Privacy Policy (タスクボード — ロック画面とホーム画面で見えるToDo)

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**  
**Operator: Trierr** (Japanese reading: トリエラ / Toriera)  
The App is operated by an individual. Trierr is the operator's trade name / brand name.

This policy explains how information is handled in the Android app “タスクボード — ロック画面とホーム画面で見えるToDo” (meaning “Task Board — ToDos visible on your lock and home screens”; the “App”). The App may appear as “タスクボード” or “Task Board” within the App or on your device.

## 1. About the App

The App organizes tasks into “Do now,” “Later,” and “Take time” categories and manages deadlines, recurrence, progress, and completion. At your request, it can display tasks in your device's lock screen or home screen wallpaper and in a home screen widget.

The App has no account registration, in-app purchases, or cloud storage operated by the operator. It has no feature that sends task content, such as titles or notes, to the operator's servers. Advertising services collect and transmit information separately, as explained in Section 3.

## 2. Information handled on your device

The App stores and uses the following information on your device to provide its features. The operator has no feature for remotely retrieving or viewing these saved data.

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Task identifiers, titles, notes, categories, deadlines, and creation times | Saving, displaying, and editing tasks |
| Completion status and times, progress percentages, and tracking method | Displaying completion, progress, and completion rates |
| Recurrence settings and series identifiers | Creating the next daily or weekly task and avoiding duplicate entries |
| Setting to keep completed tasks on the home tab | Controlling completed-task visibility on the home tab and wallpaper |
| Wallpaper position, dimensions, font size, destination, application status, automatic update pause setting, and update comparison data | Managing wallpaper previews, application, and automatic updates |
| Task data for widget and wallpaper display | Displaying and updating task titles, categories, deadlines, progress, and related information |

The App also uses your device language to select its display language, and manufacturer, brand, and installer information to show device-specific guidance. It has no feature that sends this information to the operator's servers.

The App does not explicitly disable Android automatic backup. Depending on the device, OS, and your settings, saved app data may be included in OS backups or device-to-device transfers. These are managed by the OS or backup service provider and are separate from cloud storage operated by the operator.

### Wallpaper and widget visibility

Depending on your chosen destination, the App changes only the lock screen wallpaper or both the home and lock screen wallpapers. On some devices, including Fire tablets, applying wallpaper to both screens may be necessary for it to appear on the lock screen.

Turning on “Pause automatic wallpaper updates” in Settings stops automatic wallpaper updates from task changes, device restarts, app updates, language changes, and similar events. Pausing or resuming does not change your current wallpaper or restore a previous wallpaper. You can still apply wallpaper manually while paused; doing so keeps automatic updates paused. Widgets continue updating regardless of this setting.

Wallpaper and widgets display information such as task titles, categories, deadlines, recurrence dates, and progress. When the setting to keep completed tasks on the home tab is enabled, completed tasks also appear on the wallpaper with strikethrough formatting. Note contents are not displayed on wallpaper or widgets.

Depending on the device state, others may see these displays even when the App is closed or the device is locked. Take care when including sensitive information in task titles. Wallpaper images are passed to the device's OS for application, not sent to the operator.

## 3. Advertising and third-party data processing

### 3.1 Google AdMob

The App uses Google LLC's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner ads at the bottom of the App's home tab. The App does not place ads on the lock screen wallpaper or home screen widget.

For advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, the advertising SDK may collect and transmit information and share it with Google and advertising partners. This includes IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), advertising and other identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics. The App's code does not pass task content, such as titles, notes, or deadlines, to ad requests.

For retention, processing locations, recipients, and controls for Google's data processing, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

### 3.2 Advertising choices

Android settings may allow you to delete or reset your advertising ID. Available controls vary by device and OS. These controls do not necessarily stop ads or all communication needed for ad delivery.

The current version has no dedicated screen for advertising consent or withdrawal. Publication of this policy does not itself constitute obtaining consent to personalized advertising or related processing.

## 4. Device access and analytics

- The App uses an internet connection and network status information for features such as loading ads.
- The App uses wallpaper functionality to apply wallpaper at your request and update it as tasks change.
- The App may update previously configured wallpaper after device startup, app updates, or language changes.
- Task management features do not request access to contacts, photos, documents, the camera, the microphone, or precise GPS location.

The current version does not include a separate analytics or crash reporting SDK such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Advertising SDK interaction and diagnostic data are described in Section 3.

## 5. Information received through support requests

If you contact the operator by email, the operator receives your email address and message and uses them to respond, investigate, and resolve your request. This information is retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and deleted when no longer needed. Avoid sending task content or confidential information that is not needed for your request.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Tasks and settings are stored on your device until you delete or change them. You can delete individual tasks in the App and, depending on the display setting, delete completed tasks together. Hiding completed tasks from the home tab does not delete their records.

You can delete local app data by clearing the App's data in Android settings or uninstalling the App. The operator cannot remotely delete tasks on your device. Consult your device and service settings to manage data included in OS backups or device transfers.

Applied wallpaper is managed by the OS. Wallpaper showing the previous task contents may remain after task deletion, clearing app data, or uninstalling the App. To ensure the information is removed from your wallpaper, select a different wallpaper in your device settings.

Uninstalling the App does not itself delete information already received by advertising services. For retention and deletion of data handled by Google, consult Google's policies and settings.

## 7. International processing and your rights

Advertising services may process information outside your country or region. Google's Privacy Policy explains its international transfer mechanisms and safeguards.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, object to processing of, or obtain a portable copy of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact the operator using Section 9 about information handled by the operator. For information handled by Google, you can also use the contacts and controls described in Google's policies. Where applicable, you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when App features or service providers change. Changes and the updated date will be posted on this page. Important changes will also be communicated in the App where necessary.
